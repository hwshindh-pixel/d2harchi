01 · THREE CLOUD SAAS ENGINES IN DEVELOPMENT

Focused Product Modules

Every product below is grounded in practical bottlenecks encountered by licensed architects and environmental engineers during feasibility studies and design development.

PHASE 01 · MVP IN DEVELOPMENT

CodeAudit AI

Building Code & Zoning Pre-Check Assistant

We are building a structured legal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant over Korea's Building Act, National Land Planning Act, and municipal zoning ordinances on Google Vertex AI & Gemini. The MVP focuses on helping designers verify allowable FAR/BCR, solar setback envelopes, parking requirements, and permit risks early in site feasibility.

Vertex AI / Gemini Structured Statutory RAG FAR / BCR Envelope
PHASE 02 · R&D PROTOTYPE

ThermoFlux BPS Cloud

Serverless Wrapper for EnergyPlus, Radiance & OpenFOAM

Automating container execution on Google Cloud Run and Cloud Batch so architectural teams can launch DOE EnergyPlus thermal load models, Radiance/FluxDL daylight & solar maps, and OpenFOAM wind studies directly from browser inputs—eliminating heavy local workstation requirements.

Cloud Batch DOE EnergyPlus Radiance + OpenFOAM
PHASE 03 · PLANNED RELEASE

SpatialGen Studio

Diagram-to-Massing & Area Schedule Assistant

Planned multimodal design assistant using Gemini to bridge early concept sketches, adjacency diagrams, and program requirement tables with structured 3D massing schemas and open-source CAD/BIM interoperability (IFC/DXF workflows) alongside automated gross-to-net area schedules.
