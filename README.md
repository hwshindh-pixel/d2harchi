# D2H Archi_Lab (`d2harchi.com`)

Official web platform of **D2H Archi_Lab** (**D2H Architects Inc.**), an early-stage AEC technology startup building cloud-native AI and environmental physics simulation SaaS products on Google Cloud and Gemini.

## Structure
- `index.html` — Lightweight landing hub featuring **4 Large Division Cards** (`Software & AI R&D`, `Architecture Design`, `Environmental AI`, `Spatial Art`), **Cloud SaaS Summary**, and **Leadership & Corporate Registry**.
- `software.html` — Detailed roadmap and Google Cloud & Gemini architecture for `CodeAudit AI`, `ThermoFlux BPS Cloud`, and `SpatialGen Studio`.
- `architecture.html` — Filterable portfolio showcase of all **32 Architecture Projects**.
- `environmental-ai.html` — Showcase of **9 Physics & CFD Simulation Workflows** (EnergyPlus, Radiance/FluxDL, OpenFOAM).
- `spatial-art.html` — Curated showcase of **8 Spatial Light Installations** by Yeojoo Park.
- `pricing.html` — Planned Freemium SaaS & Usage-Based Compute business model.
- `detail.html` — Dedicated dossier viewer (`?type=project|energy|art&id=...`) with multi-plate galleries and full-screen lightbox inspection.
- `assets/` — Full-resolution portfolio plates (`projects/`, `energy/`, `art/`), lightweight 560px WebP card thumbnails (`thumbs/`), stylesheet (`css/site.css`), and structured catalog/router (`js/catalog.js`, `js/app.js`).
