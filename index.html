PHASE 01 · MVP IN DEVELOPMENT

CodeAudit AI

Building Code & Zoning Pre-Check Assistant

We are building an AI regulatory pre-check tool powered by Google Vertex AI & Gemini that structures Korea's Building Act and municipal ordinances—helping architects verify allowable FAR/BCR, setback envelopes, and permit risks early in feasibility studies.

Google Gemini Ordinance RAG FAR / BCR Check
PHASE 02 · R&D PROTOTYPE

ThermoFlux BPS Cloud

Cloud Wrapper for EnergyPlus, Radiance & CFD

We are developing an automated cloud pipeline on Google Cloud Run & Batch so designers can run open-source DOE EnergyPlus thermal loads, Radiance/FluxDL daylighting, and OpenFOAM wind studies without expensive local workstations.

EnergyPlus Radiance / FluxDL OpenFOAM CFD
PHASE 03 · PLANNED RELEASE

SpatialGen Studio

Diagram-to-Massing & Area Schedule Assistant

Planned studio tool designed to connect early spatial diagrams and program tables with structured 3D massing schemas (open-source CAD/BIM interoperability) and automated floor-area schedules.

Multimodal AI Open CAD / IFC Roadmap 2027
